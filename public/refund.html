Refund Policy

Blink Pro purchases are handled by our reseller and Merchant of Record, Paddle.com - they process refund requests directly, per their own standard policy.

Last updated: November 2026

  2. 2. Requesting a refund

1. How refunds work

Because Paddle is the Merchant of Record for every Blink Pro purchase, they are also the ones who process and approve refunds - the charge on your statement is from Paddle.com, not MALTO, so Paddle is able to reverse it directly. We don't hold your payment details and can't issue a refund ourselves, but we're glad to help if something looks wrong with your subscription.

2. Requesting a refund

The fastest way is the link in the payment receipt email Paddle sent you, or Paddle's own support channel directly. If you can't find your receipt, open an issue on GitHub with the email address you subscribed with and we'll point you to the right place.

3. Cancelling vs. refunding

Cancelling Pro (from the Pro panel on the homepage, or through Paddle) stops future billing but does not itself refund the current period - you keep Pro until the period you already paid for ends. A refund is a separate request for a charge that's already happened, handled per the above.

4. Questions

Open an issue on GitHub and we'll answer there.
